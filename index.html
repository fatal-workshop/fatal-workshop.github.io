About the workshop

Short talks. Low overhead. Good conversations.

FATAL is an informal UC3M–UPM get-together designed to make it easy to learn what our colleagues are working on, exchange ideas across groups, and spot opportunities for future collaborations. Talks are deliberately short: enough time to explain a problem, an idea, or a result — and leave the deeper discussion for lunch.
